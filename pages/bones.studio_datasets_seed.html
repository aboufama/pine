BONES-SEED ‑ Skeletal Everyday Embodied Dataset

142 200  motion sequences from high-quality motion capture with semantic annotations, optimized for humanoid control research.

Watch Full Video

DATASET OVERVIEW
/ 01

Locomotion, everyday activities, object interactions, and complex whole-body behaviors — all curated specifically for humanoid robotics. Available in NVIDIA SOMA and Unitree G1 (MuJoCo compatible) formats.

/ 02

Up to 6 natural language descriptions per motion. Temporal segmentation with precise timestamps. Hierarchical categorization.

/ 03

Includes a built-in viewer — browse motions, annotations, and skeleton formats locally before integrating into your pipeline.
